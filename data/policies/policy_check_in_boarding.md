# SkyNova Check-in & Boarding Policy

## Web check-in
Opens **48 hours** before departure and closes **2 hours** before departure (domestic) / **3 hours** (international).

## Airport check-in
Counters close **60 minutes** before departure (domestic) and **75 minutes** (international).

## Boarding gate
The gate closes **25 minutes** before departure. Passengers not at the gate by then are treated as a **no-show** (see refund policy).

## Escalation guidance
Missed-gate and no-show disputes are handled per the refund policy; escalate only if there is a documented SkyNova error (e.g., wrong gate announced).
