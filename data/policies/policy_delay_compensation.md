# SkyNova Flight Delay & Cancellation Compensation Policy

This policy covers disruptions caused by SkyNova (not the passenger).

## Delay compensation
- **2 to under 3 hours:** a meal voucher worth ₹500.
- **3 hours or more:** the passenger may choose **(a) a full refund**, **(b) free rebooking on the next available flight**, or **(c) a travel credit worth 110% of the ticket value**.
- **6 hours or more, or overnight:** in addition to the above, SkyNova provides **hotel accommodation and meals**.

## Cancellation by SkyNova
If SkyNova cancels a flight, the passenger is entitled to a **full refund** or **free rebooking**, regardless of fare (including Saver).

## Force majeure / weather
For disruptions outside SkyNova's control (weather, air-traffic control, government orders), SkyNova offers **free rebooking or a travel credit**, but **cash compensation does not apply**.

## Escalation guidance
Escalate when the delay cause is disputed, when a medical emergency is involved, or when the compensation value is high and the case is ambiguous.
