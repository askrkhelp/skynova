# SkyNova Fare Rules — BUSINESS

The Business fare is our premium fare, fully flexible.

| Rule | Business |
|------|----------|
| Refundable | Yes — fully refundable, no cancellation fee, up to 1 hour before departure |
| Change / reschedule | Unlimited free changes (fare difference may apply on upgrade) |
| Cancellation window | Up to 1 hour before scheduled departure for a full refund |
| Check-in baggage | 35 kg (up to 2 pieces) |
| Cabin baggage | 10 kg, up to 2 pieces |
| Seat selection | Free — any seat, including extra legroom |
| Meal | Premium meal included |
| Priority / lounge | Priority check-in, boarding, and lounge access |

**Key point for support:** a Business ticket is **fully refundable with no fee** up to 1 hour before departure, and allows **free changes**.
