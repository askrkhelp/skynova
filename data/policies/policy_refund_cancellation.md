# SkyNova Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy governs voluntary cancellations by the passenger. Fare-specific rights are in the fare rules (Saver / Flex / Business).

## Refund eligibility by fare
- **Saver:** non-refundable. Only statutory taxes are refunded.
- **Flex:** refundable up to 2 hours before departure, minus a ₹1,500 fee.
- **Business:** fully refundable, no fee, up to 1 hour before departure.

## 24-hour free cancellation
Any ticket **cancelled within 24 hours of booking** is fully refunded **if** the booking was made at least **7 days** before departure. This applies to all fares, including Saver.

## No-show
If a passenger does not cancel and does not board (a "no-show"), **no refund** is due except statutory taxes, regardless of fare.

## Refund method & timing
Refunds are returned to the **original payment method**. Processing takes **7–14 business days** after approval. Travel credits (if chosen instead of cash) are issued instantly and valid for 12 months.

## Escalation guidance
Escalate to a human when: the fare is non-refundable but the customer cites a medical/bereavement exception; the refund value is unusually high; or there are signs of repeat-refund abuse.
