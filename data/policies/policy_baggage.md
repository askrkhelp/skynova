# SkyNova Baggage Policy

## Allowances (by fare)
- **Saver:** 15 kg check-in + 7 kg cabin (1 piece).
- **Flex:** 25 kg check-in + 7 kg cabin (1 piece).
- **Business:** 35 kg check-in (up to 2 pieces) + 10 kg cabin (up to 2 pieces).

## Excess baggage
Excess check-in baggage is charged at **₹600 per extra kg**, payable at the airport or pre-booked online at a discount.

## Prohibited in cabin
Power banks over 100 Wh, sharp objects, flammable liquids, and lithium batteries above the permitted limit are not allowed in cabin baggage.

## Lost baggage
Report lost baggage **within 7 days** of arrival. SkyNova provides interim expenses of ₹1,500 for essentials while tracing. Liability for lost baggage is limited to **₹20,000** unless a higher value was declared and a fee paid at check-in.

## Damaged baggage
Report damaged baggage **at the airport within 24 hours** of arrival, with the tag and photos. Routine wear and tear (scuffs, minor scratches) is not covered.

## Escalation guidance
Route lost/damaged baggage claims to the **Baggage team**. Escalate to a human when the declared value is high or the claim conflicts with the delivery scan.
