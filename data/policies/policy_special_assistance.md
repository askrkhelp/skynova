# SkyNova Special Assistance Policy

SkyNova provides assistance for passengers with reduced mobility, medical needs, expectant mothers, and unaccompanied minors.

## How to request
Requests should be made **at least 48 hours** before departure via the support desk or manage-booking page.

## Categories
- **Wheelchair assistance:** available on request, no charge.
- **Medical conditions / oxygen / stretcher:** require a **medical clearance form (MEDIF)**; these cases must be reviewed by a human agent.
- **Expectant mothers:** after 28 weeks, a doctor's certificate is required; after 36 weeks travel is not permitted.
- **Unaccompanied minors (5–12 years):** a paid escort service applies and must be arranged with a human agent.

## Escalation guidance
**Always escalate special-assistance requests involving medical clearance, unaccompanied minors, or late-pregnancy travel to a human agent** — these cannot be auto-decided.
